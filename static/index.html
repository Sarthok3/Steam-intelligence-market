What the data says

Most Steam games are never recommended. Only twelve percent get even one. Price, genre and timing decide who gets noticed.

01

Higher price tiers get noticed more

Games priced $30+ average about 6,148 recommendations. Free games average about 96. Correlation, not proof: bigger studios also charge more.

Most games are never recommended

Only 12% of all games have at least one recommendation and the median is zero. Just 0.7% of free games get any, versus 44–47% at $20 and above.
